Velvet Night Theme dresses the browser in a deep, low-lit surface. A wine frame carries the window and tab strip, a muted plum toolbar and bookmark bar sit just below it, and a dark plum new-tab page keeps the reading area quiet. Dusty rose and powder-pink text colours are tuned so tabs, toolbar, bookmarks and the address bar stay comfortable to read on every surface, and a deeper wine frame keeps private windows distinct. Every layer is painted as one solid colour, so the window stays flat and uncluttered, and the whole palette avoids bright whites for late-evening browsing.
